## IN PROGRESS — band edge blur asymmetry

User report: "the bottom of the big aurora bands is less blurred than the
top edge." (One of 3 complaints; list: 1=smeared scanlines DONE, 2=gradient
banding NOT done, 4=roll sweep too visible NOT done.)

Investigation findings (headless Playwright pixel measurements):

1. Rendered (blurred) composite: bright band peak half-max widths are
   asymmetric — top edge ~19px, bottom ~9px → the bottom IS sharper.
2. Raw (pre-blur) canvas already asymmetric: top ~15rawpx, bottom ~3rawpx —
   asymmetry comes from the SOURCE, not the CSS blur.
3. `destination-in` vertical mask test on a single centered band (exact prod
   mask + flat rect clip): expected 115px fades at 0..0.3 and 0.7..1.0, but
   measured fades collapse to ~16px at top and ~32px at bottom, and full
   plateau between — the mask behaves far more sharply than the
   `0→0.3→1, 0.7→1→0` stops should produce. Since the test used the SAME
   vGrad construction as prod, a mask bug/quirk is suspected; needs a
   definitive micro-test (gradient alone vs full pipeline) before fixing.
